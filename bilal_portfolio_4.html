About

The person behind the campaigns

I'm Mohamed Bilal, a digital marketer based in Kerala, India. I hold a B.Com in Travel and Tourism from Ideal Arts and Science College, University of Calicut, and moved into digital marketing after an internship at Acodez IT Solutions — where I got hands-on with real Meta Ads campaigns instead of just studying the theory.

Since then I've worked across Meta Ads, social media management, SEO content, and brand setup for a growing list of small and local businesses — from organics brands to battery shops to catering services. I like marketing that can be measured: a lead, a sale, a follow, a click.

Outside of marketing, I'm also drawn to the aviation and airport industry — there's something about the precision of how it all runs on time that I find genuinely appealing.
